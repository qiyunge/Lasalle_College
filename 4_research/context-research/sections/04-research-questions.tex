
\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:research-questions}

The broader research investigates how evidence position, context length, and
distractor semantic similarity affect information utilization in modern large
language models.

The study prioritizes two primary research questions, with a third question
reserved for a potential extension.

\subsection{Primary Research Questions}

\textbf{RQ1 -- Positional Effect}

How does the position of relevant information within the context affect
retrieval accuracy in modern large language models?


\noindent\textbf{RQ2 -- Context Length Interaction}

How does increasing context length influence the relationship between evidence
position and retrieval accuracy?

\subsection{Extended Research Question}

\textbf{RQ3 -- Information Interference}

How do different forms of information interference,
such as semantic similarity and repetition, affect 
the utilization of relevant evidence across context
positions and lengths?

RQ1 and RQ2 constitute the primary experimental scope. RQ3 represents a planned
extension that may be investigated if time and computational resources permit.
